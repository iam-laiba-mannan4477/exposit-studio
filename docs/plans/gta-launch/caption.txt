How do you ship 212 GB at midnight?

GTA 6 needs up to 212 GB, and Take-Two reported 32.5 million copies sold before release. How a launch like this avoids one giant midnight spike:
1. Move the bytes early: preloads opened on 10 November, nine days before launch.
2. Serve them from CDNs: copies cached on edge servers near players.
3. Stagger the spike: the unlock rolled through midnight local time, starting in New Zealand.
4. Ship deltas: the first patch was about 1.07 GB on PS5.
And find the one dependency everyone hits at once: hours before launch, PlayStation Network struggled, and a downloaded game can still need a licence check to start.

Comcast said the first 48 hours of preloads were the largest single event in its network history.
This is how a launch like this works, from public reporting; it is not Rockstar's internal architecture.
